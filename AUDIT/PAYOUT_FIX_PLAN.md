## 5. Model Lineage Confirmation

- Newer/monthly-referral model: `AffiliatePayoutLedger`.
  - It stores `affiliateId`, `userId`, `month`, `amount`, `status`, `stripeTransferId`, and unique `idempotencyKey` in `models/AffiliatePayoutLedger.ts:4`.
  - Default amount is `$12.50` at `models/AffiliatePayoutLedger.ts:25`.
  - It is created by `processReferralLinkMonthlyPayout` in the verified Stripe webhook at `pages/api/stripe/webhook.ts:571` and by the monthly annual-user cron at `pages/api/cron/process-affiliate-payouts.ts:120`.
- Legacy/mutable-balance model: `AffiliatePayout`.
  - It stores payout attempts against `Affiliate.payoutDue` with statuses `queued | sent | failed` at `models/AffiliatePayout.ts:4` and `models/AffiliatePayout.ts:28`.
  - It is used by the flat `payoutDue` sweepers/manual routes and inline webhook autopayout.
- Legacy mutable balance: `Affiliate.payoutDue`.
  - Defined as dollars in `models/Affiliate.ts:55` and schema default at `models/Affiliate.ts:133`.
  - It is the shared source for the duplicate payout paths above.
